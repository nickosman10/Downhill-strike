# CC0 asset drop

Everything in this folder is CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Each item's licence was checked on its source page when it was downloaded
(27 Nov 2026). No attribution is required; the credits below are a courtesy.

Changes made to the originals are noted per pack. Nothing else was edited.

  only offered through a Google Drive folder, and Drive refused every file
  with its "too many users have downloaded this file" limit. Source to retry:
  https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemonsters.html
- **ambientCG cracked concrete**: no matching CC0 material came up in the
  search; Poly Haven's `cracked_concrete` and `concrete_debris` cover it.
- **A dedicated building-collapse sound**: none found under CC0 alone. The
  wood-destruction set, `rumble.ogg` and the Kenney impact sounds are the
  closest.
