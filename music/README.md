# Your soundtrack

Downhill Strike plays **your own music files** while you race, shuffled, and
keeps playing between the menu and races.

Easiest: in the menu press **Choose music folder…** and pick the folder
where your songs already are (sub-folders/albums are scanned too). Or drop
`.ogg` or `.mp3` files into this folder (or into the `music` folder next
to the exported game, or `user://music`). Keys: **M** next song, **N** music
on/off.

Songs are not included with the game: the Eurobeat songs used in Initial D
are copyrighted (Avex / Super Eurobeat), so they can't be redistributed.
Files you put here are ignored by git, so they never get uploaded to the repo.

## Where to get them

DRM-free MP3 downloads work: e.g. Amazon Music (MP3 purchase), OTOTOY,
Bandcamp. Streaming apps (Spotify, Apple Music) are locked to their app and
won't work. iTunes .m4a files need converting to .mp3 first.
